# Rusher Pressure Productivity

A metric that rates pass rushers on how much they disrupt a quarterback's throw without recording a hit, hurry, or sack. Built using NFL Next Gen Stats tracking data (10Hz player positioning) and PFF scouting data from the 2021 season.

By combining QB release time deviation, pocket displacement, rusher proximity/closing speed, and play outcome, we surface defenders who generate real pressure that traditional stats miss entirely. Players like Dee Ford and Dont'a Hightower rank in the top 10 despite having some of the lowest PFF pressure counts in the dataset.

Designed for defensive coaches evaluating pass rush personnel packages, scouts and recruiters identifying undervalued edge talent, and front offices making contract decisions. Ghost pressure gives a fuller picture of a player's impact within their personnel grouping, separating the guys who quietly wreck plays from those who just rack up counting stats.
